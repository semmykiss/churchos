     1→// ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     2→// Grace ChMS — Church Management Platform (local build)
     3→// Data model adapted from 04-architecture.md §4 (40 domain tables)
     4→// Conventions honored:
     5→//   • tenant_id (orgId) on every tenant-owned table, indexed
     6→//   • money = integer minor units (cents) + currency code, never floats
     7→//   • soft delete on people/groups/messages/files; money & attendance immutable
     8→//   • append-only audit_log
     9→//   • posted gifts immutable — refunds/corrections are reversal records
    10→//   • person (not household) is the subject of attendance & giving
    11→// SQLite (local, Windows-friendly). UUIDs stored as text ids.
    12→// ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    13→
